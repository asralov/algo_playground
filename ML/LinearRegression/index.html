Linear Regression

What is Linear Regression? It is a supervised machine learning algorithm used to find the line of best fit. It models the relationship between the inputs ($X$) and outputs ($Y$) by calculating the optimal weights: slope ($m$) and intercept ($b$).

What is the Learning Rate? It determines the step size taken during weight optimization. Optimal values range from 0.01 to 0.1.

How to use: Click or tap directly on the canvas to add your custom points. Alternatively, click Add Random Points. Set your hyperparameters, and then click Train Model.
