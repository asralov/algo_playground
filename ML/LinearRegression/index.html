What does the line predict?

Predictions can extend beyond the plotted 0–1 area.

One epoch, three actions.

  1. Predict: compute ŷ = mx + b for every point.
  2. Measure: MSE = mean((ŷ − y)²).
  3. Update: move slope and intercept against the loss gradient.

R² can be negative when the model is worse than predicting the mean. It is undefined when all observed Y values are equal. The dashed reference is the exact least-squares solution, not another training run.
