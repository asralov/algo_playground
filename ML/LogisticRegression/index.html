How learning works

  1. Score: z = w₁X₁ + w₂X₂ + b.
  2. Probability: sigmoid(z) maps the score to 0–1.
  3. Learn: gradient descent adjusts the weights to reduce binary cross-entropy.

With these two raw features, the decision boundary is straight. It cannot separate XOR perfectly. Probabilities near 0.5 indicate uncertainty; confidence does not guarantee correctness.
