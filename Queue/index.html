      WHY A QUEUE?

      Think of a line at a café.

      People join at the back, and the person who arrived first is served next. A queue follows the same rule: first in, first out.

      Enqueue(value)Add a value at the rear.

      Dequeue()Remove and return the front value.

      Peek()Read the front without changing the order.

      Used in BFS, print jobs, and task scheduling. This array-backed implementation uses shift() for dequeue, which takes O(n). A queue with head/tail pointers can avoid shifting items.
