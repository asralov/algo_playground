    WHY A TRIE?

    Find the prefix. Explore below it.

    1. Start at the root. It represents the empty prefix.
    2. Follow one edge per letter. Searching “car” follows c → a → r. Other branches are skipped.
    3. Collect words below that node. A double ring means a word ends here. “car” can be a word and still lead to “care”.

    Finding a prefix of length p takes O(p) child lookups. Listing completions takes additional work to visit their branches and produce the words.
